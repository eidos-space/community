## Design Context

### Users

Eidos users looking for plugins and developers exploring or submitting plugins are equally important. The site helps both groups inspect the open community catalog, understand a plugin, and reach its source or submission guide.

### Brand Personality

Confident, precise, and open. The interface should feel calm and trustworthy while giving Eidos a recognizable visual presence.

### Aesthetic Direction

Follow Eidos Lite's neutral white and cool gray surfaces, precise type, and blue green accent. Use an almost borderless visual language: distinguish sections through proximity, alignment, whitespace, type contrast, and restrained surface changes. The homepage can use a centered, symmetrical composition. Plugin detail pages use a conventional store layout: a left aligned product summary, primary content column, and right metadata sidebar.

### Design Principles

- Give finding plugins and submitting plugins clear, visible paths.
- Use the main Eidos site's geometric mark and Eidos wordmark in the header and favicon.
- Keep the homepage introduction compact enough that plugin cards appear in the first viewport on desktop and mobile.
- Let content and typography create hierarchy before adding decoration.
- Apply repetition, proximity, contrast, and alignment consistently; reserve borders for functional reading aids such as focus states and dense tables.
- Center the homepage sections while keeping detail pages left aligned and easy to scan.
- Present plugins as simple cards directly below search. Keep small catalogs searchable without view or category controls; show broad category labels on each plugin.
- Use consistent, subtle interaction feedback and accessible focus states.
- Keep every piece of plugin information available on small screens in a natural reading order.
- Maintain the same structure and quality in English and Chinese.
